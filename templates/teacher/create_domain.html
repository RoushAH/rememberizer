Requirements:

  • Domain name must be unique
  • At least 2 fields required
  • At least 4 facts required (for multiple choice questions)
  • Each fact must have all specified fields
  • Domain will be private to your organization by default
  • You can publish it later to make it available to all teachers

Using images:

Prefix a field value with img: to make it an image. Images work anywhere text does — they can be what the question shows, or what the four answer choices show.

{"name": "Erato", "symbol": "Lyre", "portrait": "img:erato.png"}
{"name": "Clio",  "symbol": "Scroll", "portrait": "img:https://example.org/clio.png"}
  • img:erato.png — upload the file below; the name must match
  • img:https://... — loaded from that URL; nothing to upload
  • Allowed types: PNG, JPG, GIF, WebP. Max 5MB per image, 25MB per upload
  • Give every fact an image for a field, or the answer choices will mix pictures and text

How it works:

Point at a roster export and its photo gallery. Each row becomes one fact: the first column is matched to a photo file and replaced by it, and every other column becomes something to quiz.

Roster CSV:

ManagementSystemID,Surname,Forename,YearGroup,TutorGroup
10482,Wright,Alex,Year 9,9B
10483,Okafor,Chidi,Year 9,9B

Photo folder:

photos/10482.jpg
photos/10483.jpg

Gives you facts like:

{"photo": "<10482.jpg>", "surname": "Wright", "forename": "Alex",
 "year_group": "Year 9", "tutor_group": "9B"}

Notes:

  • Matching ignores case and the file extension: 10482 matches 10482.JPG
  • Rows with no matching photo are skipped and reported, not rejected
  • Photos no row names are never uploaded or stored
  • At least 4 rows must match a photo, for multiple-choice questions
  • Headers are tidied into field names: YearGroup is asked about as “year group”
  • Allowed image types: PNG, JPG, GIF, WebP. Max 5MB per photo, 25MB per upload
  • The domain is private to your organization until you publish it — keep photo domains unpublished
